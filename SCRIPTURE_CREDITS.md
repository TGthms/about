## Japanese — リビングバイブル (JCB), 2016

Psalm 23 and the footer's 2 Timothy 1:7 use the current Japanese Living /
Contemporary Bible (リビングバイブル; JCB on YouVersion). Its publisher's
quotation guidance permits up to 500 verses, with less than one quarter of the
work's text and the prescribed acknowledgment. This site quotes seven Japanese
verses, alongside six NIV verses, within that limited quotation allowance.

The edition combines verses 2–3; the reader preserves that numbering and the
published wording. It does not invent a separate verse boundary.

> Scripture quotations taken from the Japanese Living Bible (リビングバイブル).
> Copyright © 1978, 2011, 2016 by Biblica, Inc.®
> Used by permission. All rights reserved worldwide.

- [Publisher and quotation terms](https://www.biblegateway.com/versions/Japanese-Living-Bible-JLB/)

JCB’s explicit quotation terms are the basis for this use. A copyright term
having expired in Japan does not alone establish public-domain status in the
