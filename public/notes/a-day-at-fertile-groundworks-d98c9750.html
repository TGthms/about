A Day at Fertile GroundWorks

VolunteeringCommunity

Written in English

Yesterday, I had the opportunity to volunteer at Fertile GroundWorks with some friends.

It was a great experience to give back to the community and learn more about sustainable gardening. The garden grows fresh produce that helps support local organizations like Tri-Valley Haven to provide food for people in need.

I loved seeing how faith can be put into action through caring for the community and serving others.

No photos this time, but plenty of good memories!

Grateful for the experience and looking forward to more opportunities to give back!
